# CATTLEX Machine Learning Scientific Methodology & Validation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

**CATTLEX** (*An AI-Integrated, Solar-Powered IoT System Enhanced with Predictive Analytics for Multi-Disease Management in Livestock*) incorporates a rigorously evaluated, duplicate-safe Machine Learning inference service for multi-disease bovine differential diagnosis.

The selected and locked production disease classification algorithm is:
**RandomForestClassifier (CATTLEX-RF-v1)**

---

## 2. Dataset Distribution & Pattern Analysis

The foundational dataset contains:
- **Total recorded rows:** 2,044
- **Binary clinical symptom features:** 93
- **Target bovine disease classes:** 26
- **Unique symptom patterns:** 433

### Critical Scientific Principle: Duplicate Symptom Pattern Isolation
In clinical livestock disease tabular datasets, identical symptom profiles frequently appear dozens or hundreds of times due to synthetic oversampling, naive multi-source data aggregation, or repeated identical recording of cardinal symptoms.

Evaluating ML models using standard random k-fold cross-validation directly on the raw 2,044 rows results in severe **data leakage** (identical symptom vectors present simultaneously in both training and test folds). This artificially inflates test performance to 99.9%–100% and masks true generalization deficits.

To establish authentic scientific validity:
1. All rows were clustered into **433 unique symptom vectors**.
2. A strict **duplicate-safe split** was enforced:
   - **Training Set:** 346 unique symptom patterns (80%)
   - **Independent Hold-out Test Set:** 87 unique symptom patterns (20%)
   - **Train/Test Feature Vector Overlap:** Exactly **0**

---

## 3. Cross-Validation & Model Selection

Model selection was executed using **Repeated Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation**:
- **Folds:** 5 splits
- **Repeats:** 10 repeats (50 total validation fits per model)
- **Primary Selection Metric:** Macro-Averaged F1-Score (Macro-F1) to account for class imbalance across unique symptom patterns.

### Why Random Forest Was Selected
Across repeated cross-validation and independent evaluation, Random Forest demonstrated superior robustness on complex, high-dimensional binary vectors with zero susceptibility to feature scale anomalies:
- Handles high non-linear feature interactions between physiological symptoms (e.g. fever + udder inflammation vs. fever + respiratory distress).
- Native Gini feature importance enables interpretable veterinary explainability.
- Fast, deterministic prediction latency.

### Why Alternative Approaches Were Rejected
- **SMOTENC Rejection:** Applying SMOTE or SMOTENC on high-dimensional binary vectors creates synthetic non-existent combinations of clinical symptoms that do not correspond to viable bovine pathologies.
- **Original 26-Row `Testing.csv` Rejection:** The original 26-row `Testing.csv` represents an arbitrarily sampled single vector per class with zero variance and cannot serve as a statistically sound generalization test.

---

## 4. Final Production Model Hyperparameters

The serialized production model bundle (`cattlex_rf_artifacts/cattlex_rf_model.joblib`) was trained with the following exact configuration:

```python
RandomForestClassifier(
    n_estimators=500,
    max_depth=16,
    min_samples_split=2,
    min_samples_leaf=1,
    max_features="log2",
    criterion="gini",
    class_weight="balanced",
    random_state=42
)
```

- **Features (93):** Binary presence/absence of clinical bovine symptoms (preserved in exact alphabetical/training order).
- **Classes (26):** Anthrax, Blackleg, Bloat, Bovine Viral Diarrhea, Brucellosis, Calf Diphtheria, Calf Scours, Foot and Mouth Disease, Foot Rot, Ketosis, Liver Fluke, Lumpy Skin Disease, Mastitis, Metritis, Milk Fever, Pink Eye, Pneumonia, Rabies, Ringworm, Salmonellosis, Theileriosis, Traumatic Reticuloperitonitis, Tuberculosis, Wooden Tongue, Bovine Respiratory Disease, Hypocalcemia.

## 6. Model Confidence vs. Veterinary Clinical Assessment

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Safety & Interpretability Mandate:**
> Output probabilities generated by the Random Forest classifier represent **model prediction confidence**, NOT a confirmed clinical veterinary diagnosis.
> 
> Confidence thresholds:
> - **HIGH:** Confidence $\ge 0.80$
> - **MODERATE:** $0.50 \le \text{Confidence} < 0.80$
> - **LOW:** $\text{Confidence} < 0.50$
>
> In the user interface and API outputs, predictions are strictly accompanied by the disclaimer:
> *"AI decision-support prediction — veterinary clinical assessment required."*

---

## 7. Decoupling Disease Prediction from Health-Risk Scoring

CATTLEX explicitly distinguishes between:
1. **Multi-Disease Symptom Classification (ML):** Probabilistic disease matching based on observed clinical symptoms (e.g., Mastitis with 91% model confidence).
2. **Physiological Health-Risk Engine (Rule-Based & Transparent):** Real-time 0–100 risk scoring computed from continuous collar telemetry (temperature, heart rate, respiration, activity, feed/water intake, THI heat stress index).
   - $0 - 35$: **HEALTHY**
   - $36 - 70$: **AT_RISK**
   - $71 - 100$: **CRITICAL**
